<p>CamperLog is an app for recording camper trips, their costs, and the currencies they were paid in, together with your vehicle's data sheet, maintenance, and logbook.</p>
<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Tour log: destination, dates, trip type (day trip, weekend, vacation), distance, overnight stays, and costs; searchable and filterable by year, with the tour's stops as a timeline.</li>
<li>Stops: overnight places, water &amp; waste, fuel &amp; charging, tolls, sightseeing, food, ferries and more, each with costs by category; electricity as flat, metered or coin billing, calculated for you. Overnight stops keep pitch details and a "would return" flag. Describe places in text or paste coordinates and map links (parsed offline); geo: links from map apps open a new stop. With the optional Location feature, "Use current location" fills in coordinates on the spot. With the optional Weather &amp; map feature, "Get weather" fetches a one-time snapshot from Open-Meteo for today's stops, and a tour's or the Stops tab's map shows its located stops on an OpenStreetMap background.</li>
<li>Multiple vehicles: a data sheet per vehicle (general data, purchase &amp; sale, insurance &amp; tax, dimensions &amp; weight, engine, tyres, tanks, energy, notes), a "Breakdown &amp; accident" card with tap-to-dial numbers, "Where am I?" for your current coordinates (with Location on), and the remaining payload from a measured empty weight; switch between vehicles or view tours and the overview for all of them together.</li>
<li>Logbook: one tap for "Today" records the cassette and grey water emptying, a gas bottle swap or a diesel or gas heater run, with history and undo. Ticking these on a stop records them for you.</li>
<li>Photos on stops, repairs and logbook entries, and a document wallet per vehicle with expiry reminders.</li>
<li>Maintenance and repairs: next inspection, gas check and leak test dates, last oil change with its odometer reading, and a repair log with date, description, mileage, and cost.</li>
<li>Reminders: cards on the vehicle tab flag upcoming maintenance; lead time and the oil-change interval are configurable in settings. Notifications are optional (off by default): turning them on runs a daily background check for due maintenance and a backup reminder, with no exact alarms.</li>
<li>Multiple currencies: costs keep their original currency and precision; amounts are entered in your device's number format.</li>
<li>Overview: tours, distance, travel days, overnight stays, and costs per currency, in total and per year, for one vehicle or all of them; optionally converted into a main currency with your own exchange rates.</li>
<li>Export: tours and stops as CSV for spreadsheets.</li>
<li>Backup and restore: one ZIP file with all data, photos and documents; restoring shows a preview first. Optional backup folder with one-tap or automatic backups and a reminder.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Privacy:</b> CamperLog works completely offline and needs no account. Three optional features are off by default: "Use current location" reads your position once when you tap it (location permission, never in the background), "Weather &amp; map" fetches a one-time weather snapshot from Open-Meteo for today's stops and loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap for the area you are viewing (internet permission, used only when turned on), and "Notifications" run a daily background check for due maintenance and the backup reminder entirely on your device (notification permission; plus two no-prompt permissions to restart the check after a reboot and keep the device briefly awake, used only while notifications are on). All data stays on your device until you export or share it yourself.</p>
<p>Uninstalling the app deletes its data, so save a backup regularly, for example before switching phones.</p>
