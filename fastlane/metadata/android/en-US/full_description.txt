<p>CamperLog records your camper trips with their costs in any currency, plus your vehicle's data sheet, maintenance, and logbook.</p>
<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Tour log: destination, dates, trip type (day trip, weekend, vacation), distance, overnight stays, costs, and countries detected from its stops; searchable and filterable by year, with the tour's stops as a timeline and a diary per travel day.</li>
<li>Stops: overnight places, water &amp; waste, fuel &amp; charging, tolls, sightseeing, food, ferries and more, each with costs by category; electricity as flat, metered or coin billing, calculated for you. Overnight stops keep pitch details and a "would return" flag. Describe places in text or paste coordinates and map links (parsed offline); geo: links from map apps open a new stop. With the optional Location feature, "Use current location" fills in coordinates on the spot. With the optional Weather &amp; map feature, "Get weather" fetches a one-time snapshot from Open-Meteo for today's stops, and maps show located stops on OpenStreetMap.</li>
<li>Multiple vehicles: a data sheet per vehicle (from purchase and insurance to tyres and tanks), a "Breakdown &amp; accident" card with tap-to-dial numbers, "Where am I?" (with Location on), and the remaining payload from a measured empty weight; switch between vehicles or view tours and the overview for all of them together.</li>
<li>Logbook: one tap for "Today" records the cassette and grey water emptying, a gas bottle swap or a diesel or gas heater run, with history and undo. Ticking these on a stop records them for you.</li>
<li>Photos on stops, repairs and logbook entries, and a document wallet per vehicle with expiry reminders.</li>
<li>Maintenance and repairs: next inspection, gas check and leak test dates, last oil change with its odometer reading, and a repair log with costs.</li>
<li>Reminders: cards on the vehicle tab flag upcoming maintenance, with configurable lead time and oil-change interval; optional notifications (off by default) from a daily check, with no exact alarms.</li>
<li>Checklists (departure, arrival, winterizing …) from your own templates, ticked off per tour or vehicle; full-text search across all data.</li>
<li>Multiple currencies: costs keep their currency and precision; amounts use your device's number format.</li>
<li>Overview: tours, distance, travel days, overnight stays, countries, and costs per currency, in total and per year, for one vehicle or all of them; optionally converted into a main currency with your own exchange rates.</li>
<li>Export: tours and stops as CSV; one tour as a ZIP with a printable HTML page, Markdown, GPX waypoints, CSV and photos.</li>
<li>Backup and restore: one ZIP file with all data, photos and documents; restoring shows a preview first. Optional backup folder with one-tap or automatic backups and a reminder.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Privacy:</b> CamperLog works completely offline and needs no account. Four optional features are off by default: "Use current location" reads your position once when you tap it (location permission), "Track recording" saves a tour's route only while you record it, with a permanent notification, "Weather &amp; map" fetches a one-time weather snapshot from Open-Meteo, loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and looks up a place you search for via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (internet permission, used only when turned on), and "Notifications" run a daily background check for due maintenance and the backup reminder entirely on your device (notification permission; two no-prompt permissions for restart after reboot and a brief wake lock, used only then). All data stays on your device until you export or share it yourself.</p>
<p>Uninstalling the app deletes its data, so save a backup regularly, for example before switching phones.</p>
